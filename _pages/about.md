I am a final year undergraduate at the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay, studying electrical engineering, with a bit of condensed matter physics and materials science on the side. My primary interests are in nanofabrication (['there's plenty of room at the bottom'](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/There%27s_Plenty_of_Room_at_the_Bottom)) and manufacturing, but I am pretty enthusiastic about anything which involves building. 

I am currently working on combining cutting-edge packaging techniques with flexible electronic platforms to make flexible substrates with densely packed embedded chips for readout. This work is at the [DESH Group](https://www.linkedin.com/company/devices-and-system-heterointegration-group-iitbombay/) at IIT Bombay and IITBNF as my Master's thesis where I am working with Matangi Sricharan under Prof. Veeresh Deshpande's guidance. The work is in collaboration with Prof. Prosenjit Sen's group at IISc and Prof. Subramaniam Iyer's group at UCLA. 

Besides, I founded [HackerFab IITB](https://abhineet-agarwal.github.io/projects/hackerfab/) - India's first student-led chip fab, where we design and build our own tools from scratch. Swarajya recently wrote about it: [Inside HackerFab IITB, India's First Student-Led Chip Fab](https://swarajyamag.com/technology/inside-hackerfab-iitb-indias-first-student-led-chip-fab).

I also maintain [OpenTCAD](https://abhineet-agarwal.github.io/projects/opentcad/), an open-source Python framework for semiconductor process and device simulation. It's an attempt at a scriptable alternative to closed commercial TCAD. It is early and I would welcome contributors, particularly on material parameter files, physics models and worked examples. The code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/abhineet-agarwal/opentcad); do reach out if any of it sounds interesting.

You can find some of the stuff I have worked on previously [here](https://abhineet-agarwal.github.io/projects/). 


Have always found it difficult to be put in a box. Non-exhaustive list of things I am currently into:
- Interfacing AI with the real world
- Semiconductor Devices and VLSI Technology
- Manufacturing, especially in Tier-2 India
- AI + EDA, and automating process engineering
- The future of personal devices
- Developmental Economics

Besides work, I love music - trained as a vocalist, trying out new sports and backpacking. I also avidly follow and read about politics, history and the technology landscape here in India and the world. You can find my reading list [here](https://www.goodreads.com/user/show/52986688-abhineet-agarwal), and some of my writing [here](https://abhineet-agarwal.github.io/blog/).
